Northfield University - MS in Computer Science (sample data, fictional)

Admissions overview
The Master of Science in Computer Science admits students for the Fall term only.

Key dates
- Round 1 application deadline: January 5, 2027, 11:59 PM EST
- Round 2 application deadline: March 1, 2027
- Financial aid priority date: January 15, 2027
Decisions are released in rolling batches after each round.

Required application materials
- Official transcripts from every university attended
- Updated resume or CV
- Statement of purpose (maximum 2 pages)
- Three letters of recommendation, at least two from academic referees
- GRE general test scores are optional for the current cycle
- TOEFL or IELTS scores are required unless you completed your bachelor's degree at an English-medium institution
- Application fee of $90, waived for applicants who qualify for a fee waiver
- A copy of your passport is required for international applicants only
- Portfolio of research or projects is optional but recommended

Eligibility
- A bachelor's degree in computer science or a closely related field is required
