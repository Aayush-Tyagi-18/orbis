Orbis

Paperwork, pulled into orbit.

Orbis reads a university’s requirements page, lists what it asks for and when it is due, and checks your documents against it. Every item points to the exact line it came from.

1. It reads

Paste a link, upload a PDF or drop in text. Orbis pulls out requirements and deadlines.

PDF, web page, text

2. It checks itself

Each item must quote the source word for word. If the quote can’t be found, it is flagged, never shown as fact.

Quote and page shown

3. It tells you what’s missing

Your documents are matched to the checklist. You see what is done, expired, missing or needs your review.
